Board Sign Fit Lab — 24″ wide

Each control is a design rule that changes how much room trail names get on a 24″ board. The counts are measured against every name in the trail inventory. Rows outlined in red don't fit at the chosen cap height; type never shrinks. Arrows, symbols and badges keep the same physical size whichever typeface is picked, so only the names change. Boards come in three sizes only: 24″ × 12″ (1–2 rows), 24″ × 16″ (3 rows), 24″ × 24″ (4–5 rows).
