Board standard (from the Fit Lab)

  • Three board sizes, all 24″ wide. 24×12 (1–2 rows), 24×16 (3 rows), 24×24 (4–5 rows).
  • Standard margins and header. 1″ from trim to content on every side. Header is always 3″ on a solid black band: the gold brandmark at 3″ tall with HAWK PRIDE OFFROAD beside it in white at 1.25″ caps. Entries are spread evenly over the space below the header (at least 3.25″ each), separated by a 0.1″ gray rule that runs margin to margin.
  • One letter size. Names in Anton caps at 1.5″ cap height, never shrunk. Readable to about 45–60 ft.
  • Arrows in one column at the right. Every row has the same name space. Rows run straight, then left, then right.
  • Numbered trail symbols. Number inside the difficulty shape, at the left of each row.
  • Green Line badge beside the name. Outfitter Trail (100) carries the badge wherever it's on the route.
  • No “To” prefix. The arrow already means “this way to”.
  • Full-bleed black border. 0.5″ black runs to the edge of the board, then 0.5″ clear to the content. Printer adds 0.125″ bleed (blue line in print guides). Sign ID printed in the frame.
  • Street signs unchanged. 6″ tall, 24″ or 30″ wide by name length, Green Line plaque under Outfitter Trail.
