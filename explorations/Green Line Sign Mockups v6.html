Green Line — Sign Mockups v6

Board standard

  • Boards drawn by the design system. Every board here is the standard BoardSign component, the same one the SVG print export uses.
  • Fixed 4″ entry. Each entry is one 4″ band. Board size follows the entry count: 24×6 (1), 24×12 (2), 24×16 (3), 24×20 (4), 24×24 (5).
  • Header. Solid black band to 3.5″: white brandmark 2.15″ tall with HAWK PRIDE OFFROAD at 1.35″ caps, centered as a pair. One-entry boards have no header.
  • Margins. 1″ top and sides; 0.5″ under the last entry. Nothing is closer than 1″ to the edge.
  • Names. Barlow Semi Condensed Bold, mixed case, 1.5″ cap height, never shrunk. No “To” prefix.
  • Each entry. Numbered trail symbol · name · inline Green Line badge (if on route) · arrow in the right column. Rows run straight, then left, then right. 0.1″ gray rule between entries.
  • Border. 0.5″ black to the edge. Sign ID in the bottom border.
  • Street signs. 6″ tall, 24″ or 30″ wide by name length, Green Line plaque under Outfitter Trail.
