2aList Board

36″ × 36″ board (widened from 24″ for longer trail names). Built from 1c without the map. One-color black brandmark at top. Each row: trail number, then name with its difficulty symbol right beside it. Left-family arrows sit far left, all others far right. Destinations put their icon in the number column.

1aBlack Board

Black PVC board with white row panels. Arrow color carries the meaning, per the Playbook. Yellow junction header, legend and logo in the footer.

1bColor Bands

Each row is a full-width band in its difficulty color, like stacked street signs. Reads from the farthest distance. Arrows are white because the band carries the color.

1cJunction Map

A schematic of the intersection sits above the list, with trail-colored lines leaving a You Are Here dot. Best at complex junctions; each board needs its own diagram.

1dHawk Yellow

Brand-forward yellow board with black type and ruled rows. Symbols and arrows sit on white tiles so every difficulty color stays legible against yellow.
