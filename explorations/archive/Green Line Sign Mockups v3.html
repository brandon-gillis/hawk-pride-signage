Green Line — Sign Mockups v3

Standards applied in this pass

  • Panel sizes on a 6″ grid. Boards are 36″ wide. Height = 6″ header + 6″ per row (12, 18, 24, 30″). A board steps to 48″ wide only when a name will not fit at standard letter height.
  • One letter height on every board. Names are always 2.6″ type and never shrink to fit, so every sign reads at the same distance.
  • Fixed row order and arrow side. Rows run straight, then left, then right. Straight and left arrows sit at the left edge and right arrows at the right edge, with columns aligned down each board.
  • White margin, inset frame. A 0.375″ black frame sits 0.25″ inside the trim. No bleed is needed and small trim errors do not show. Four 5/16″ mounting holes go through the frame.
  • Sign ID on every sign. Route code + inventory number (e.g. GL-04) is printed in the frame and matches the GIS point for that sign.
  • Green Line is a route, not a trail. Every “Green Line” in the sign inventory is Outfitter Trail (100, Easy). It is signed by its own name, number and difficulty, with the Green Line badge beside it on boards and as a plaque under its street blade.
  • Trail data from the inventory. Numbers and ratings come from the Trail Inventory sheet (new numbers).
  • Unrated trails print nothing until rated. Gray and “?” mark the gap. These signs are not ready for production.
